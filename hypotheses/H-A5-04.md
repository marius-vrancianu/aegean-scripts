## Why this pair
Under H-A5-01, English (an unrelated language) passed Test 1 at p1 = 0.009, because the matched null had too few two-letter forms (H-A5-02 outcome). The revised null of H-A5-03 is meant to remove exactly that. This pair checks whether it does.

## Hypothesis
With the English verb lexicon, M1 matches Linear A sign-groups more often than it does with length-stratified matched pseudo-lexicons (Test 1), and by a wider margin than on Linear B words of known Greek meaning (Test 2).

**Prediction, stated in advance: outcome A** (p1 > α and p2 > α).

**How each outcome will be read** (fixed now):
- **A:** the revised null removed English's Test 1 false positive, as intended.
- **B with p1 ≤ α only:** the revised null did not remove it. English then differs from length-matched noise in some other way (for example letter co-occurrence within forms). Test 1 alone stays unusable, and the next revision of the null has to address it.
- **Any outcome with p2 ≤ α:** a false positive of Test 2 for an unrelated language, as stated in H-A5-02.
