# H-A2-08 — Census of integer totals: do KU-RO and PO-TO-KU-RO add up?

- **Logged:** 2026-11-05 (the merge commit is the timestamp of record)
- **Task:** A2.8 (brought forward); proposed by Marius 2026-11-05
- **Layer:** L0. Integers only; no fraction values are used.
- **Status:** pre-registered
- **Batch:** A2 registration batch 2 = {H-A2-08}. Running count of A2 hypotheses registered: 3.

## Hypothesis
**Primary, testable:** among KU-RO totals that can be tested with integers only, more are exactly equal to the sum of their entries than a chance baseline predicts.

**Descriptive aim, equally important:** classify every KU-RO and PO-TO-KU-RO occurrence in corpus-v0.1 into the observable categories below. The aim is a transparent, reproducible account of how often Minoan totals add up as transmitted, and of why the others cannot be tested.

KI-RO is out of scope here; it belongs to H-A2-03.

## Data
- corpus-v0.1 (documents.jsonl sha256 `bb14ce5c…`).
- Every KU-RO and PO-TO-KU-RO occurrence, as found by `aegean.arithmetic.inventory`. There are 37 KU-RO and 2 PO-TO-KU-RO occurrences.
- **Side and tablet:** a lineara ID ending in `a` or `b` is one side of a tablet (e.g. `HT122a` and `HT122b` are tablet `HT122`). Any other ID is a single-sided document.

## Step 1 — GORILA check (precondition; frozen before any sum is computed)
1. **Facsimiles checked.** For every occurrence, check the GORILA facsimile of its side (lineara.xyz `images/<ID>-Facsimile.jpg` at commit 9c81beba), and the photograph where one exists.
2. **Per quantity.** For the total and for every entry under the primary scope (S1), record:
   - the corpus-v0.1 reading;
   - the facsimile reading;
   - **intact: yes / partly / no**.
3. **Per side.** Record **entry area complete: yes / no / unclear**. This asks whether a break, a missing corner or an illegible stretch could hold entries that are now lost.
4. **Disagreements.** Where the facsimile and corpus-v0.1 disagree, the facsimile reading is used and the disagreement is listed as a corpus erratum.
5. **Where it is stored.** The checks go in `experiments/A2/gorila-checks/` and are **committed before the census script computes any sum**. The script must refuse to run on an occurrence that has no committed check.
6. **Who checks.** Claude does the checking, counting strokes (Rule 5 applies). Marius may second-check a sample. Any disagreement between the two is recorded, and the more cautious judgement (less intact) is used.

## Step 2 — Scope rules
- **S1 (primary), KU-RO:** all unmarked entries on the same side, after the previous marker on that side (or the start of the side) and before the KU-RO line. This is the inventory's candidate scope.
- **S2 (alternative, reported, not decisive), KU-RO:** all unmarked entries anywhere on the same side. This covers totals written at the top of a list, or between sub-lists.
- **P1 (primary), PO-TO-KU-RO:** the sum of all KU-RO totals on all sides of the same tablet.
- **P2 (alternative), PO-TO-KU-RO:** the sum of all unmarked entries on all sides of the same tablet.

## Step 3 — Categories
Each occurrence goes into the first category that applies, under each scope separately.

| # | Category | Condition (all observable) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | untestable: no total | the marker has no quantity on its line |
| 2 | needs values | a fraction sign appears in the total or in any entry in scope. Passed to H-A2-01/02 |
| 3 | untestable: damaged | the total or any entry in scope is marked `partly` or `no` in the GORILA check |
| 4 | untestable: scope empty | there are no entries in scope |
| 5 | **consistent** | sum of entries = total |
| 6 | **near miss** | the absolute difference is exactly 1, 10, 100 or 1,000, i.e. one numeral stroke |
| 7 | **missing entries** | sum < total, and the side's entry area is recorded `no` or `unclear` in Step 1 |
| 8 | **inconsistent** | anything else |

Explanations (scribal slip, misreading, carry-over from another document, a different unit of measure) are discussed afterwards and labelled as possibilities. They are never categories.

## Step 4 — Chance baseline (primary test)
- **Testable set:** the KU-RO occurrences in categories 5–8 under S1.
- **Null:** randomly reassign the totals among the testable set, keeping each set of entries and its sum. Count "consistent" and "near miss" under each reassignment. Use 10,000 permutations with seed `20261105`.
- **p:** the share of permutations with as many or more consistent rows than observed.

## Known-script validation
None yet. The same census on Linear B *to-so* totals is A2.4 and needs DAMOS data. Until it exists, results are interpreted descriptively. The primary test is expected to pass easily, since KU-RO = "total" is near-certain. Its role is to show that the scope rule and parser recover that known answer, and to quantify how much of the corpus can be tested at all.

## Decision rule
- If fewer than 5 KU-RO occurrences reach categories 5–8 under S1: **descriptive only**. Report the table and run no test.
- **Supported:** consistent count above the null, p < 0.01.
- **Not supported:** p ≥ 0.05.
- **Inconclusive:** anything in between.
- **Always reported, whatever the decision:**
  - the full category table under S1, S2, P1 and P2;
  - the list of errata;
  - every near miss and inconsistent case, with document, sum, total and difference.
