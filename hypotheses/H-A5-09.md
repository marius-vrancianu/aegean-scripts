## Part 1 — Protocol (revision 2)

**Everything in H-A5-03, Part 1 holds without change, except the decision null, the batch and the closure of H-A5-03.** That includes H-A5-01's terms, data, both tests, length bins, α = 0.05, the decision rule with the batch correction, the deviations rule, the reporting, every seed, and H-A5-03's rule for pairs run under several protocols (point 4), extended below.

### What changes, and why
1. **Decision null: position-aware bigram-matched, length-stratified pseudo-lexicons** (`nulls.positional_bigram_matched_lexicon`). As H-A5-03's null, filled length by length to the real lexicon's expanded-form counts, but a form's letters are drawn from a first-order Markov chain fitted to the real lexicon *at each length and position*: the first letter from the real first-letter frequencies at that length, letter i from the real frequencies of the letters that follow the previous letter at that length and position i. K = 1,000, drawn in sequence from `random.Random(20261114)`.
   - *Why:* under H-A5-03, both unrelated lexicons beat length-matched noise on Greek words with M1 (English +0.033, Turkish +0.032, p ≈ 0.015; descriptive). One candidate cause is adjacent-letter co-occurrence inside forms, which H-A5-03's null does not match (its letters are independent given length and position). Another is something specific to M1 (its licences, classes, lexicon conversion or matching rule): under M2 the advantage is gone for Turkish (−0.003) and smaller for English (+0.019, p = 0.139), a change that was not tested. This revision addresses the first candidate only.
   - *Why position-aware:* H-A5-03's null matches per-position letter frequencies. A bigram null with transitions pooled over positions (`nulls.bigram_matched_lexicon`, the version first registered in this PR) gives those up (position distance to the real lexicon 0.09–0.18 against 0.06–0.08) and so changes two factors at once (review of PR #63, R1). The position-aware null keeps them (0.05–0.06) and adds adjacent-letter co-occurrence (bigram distance 0.08–0.09 against 0.14–0.26). Matching co-occurrence also makes the pseudo-lexicons reproduce more of the real forms (next bullet), and this null cannot separate the two. Chosen by Marius on 2026-11-19 after the review.
   - *Its price:* it reproduces more of the real lexicon's own forms: 0.53–0.79 of the expanded forms on average, against 0.34–0.69 for H-A5-03's null. Tests are harder to pass, and **an outcome A under this null is a weaker negative** than under H-A5-03 (review of PR #63, N3).
     - **At length 2 the null is the real lexicon.** A first-order chain over two letters is the forms' joint distribution, and the count is matched exactly, so every pseudo-lexicon's two-letter forms are exactly the real lexicon's (English and Turkish, both methods). Two-letter forms add nothing to either test.
     - Mean overlap at length 3 is 0.62–0.81 (H-A5-03's null 0.44–0.72) and at length 4 0.33–0.54 (0.02–0.36) (review of PR #63, re-review 1).
   - *What it does not match:* co-occurrence of non-adjacent letters, and anything beyond a first-order chain. With few forms at some (length, position, letter) states (Hebrew has 14 four-letter forms), the chain there is close to the real forms themselves.
2. **Batch:** this protocol's own batch (`batch-H-A5-09.md`), Benjamini–Hochberg at q = 0.05 over its pair p-values. At most 50 pairs.
3. **H-A5-03 is closed to new pairs** (its six registered pairs only; enforced by the runner since PR #61). Any later revision must close H-A5-09 in the same way.
4. **Pairs run under several protocols** are cited by the **least favourable of all their outcomes**, in the order A < B < C (this extends H-A5-03, Part 1, point 4, from two protocols to any number). A C needs C under every protocol the pair was run under.
5. **False positives are counted per protocol run, not by cited label** (review of PR #63, N2). Under point 4 every re-run pair stays cited as A whatever this batch shows, so the benchmark's false-positive tally lists each protocol's outcomes separately (`docs/benchmark.md`, results table), and an unrelated-language pair with raw p2 ≤ α (before the batch correction) under any protocol counts as a false positive of that protocol.

### Reported besides the decision
As in H-A5-03. In addition, each outcome file reports the control row (real lexicon against the position-aware bigram null on Greek words) beside the same row under H-A5-03, as the calibration of the revised null.

**A reading fixed in advance for that calibration** (descriptive, decides no outcome). For M1 × English and M1 × Turkish, compare the control excess under this null with the same row under H-A5-03 (+0.033 and +0.032):
- if both excesses fall below +0.010 and both p > 0.05: consistent with adjacent-letter co-occurrence, or with this null's larger share of real forms, accounting for most of that advantage; this null does not separate the two;
- if both stay at or above +0.020 with p ≤ 0.05: adjacent-letter co-occurrence does not account for most of it;
- anything else (one lexicon each way, or excesses between +0.010 and +0.020): inconclusive, reported as such.

No branch attributes the advantage to co-occurrence as a cause; each says only whether this null removes it. The larger share of real forms lowers the excess whatever its cause, so the second branch is the conservative one: reaching it is harder under this null than under H-A5-03's.

### Known-script validation of the revised null (before registration; lexicon side only)
`experiments/A5.17/null_validation.py` (PR #64; 100 draws per lexicon, seeds 0–99; output `results/null-validation.json`), for M1's three lexicons and M2's three:
- exact counts of expanded forms at every length, as for H-A5-03's null;
- position distance to the real lexicon 0.05–0.06 (H-A5-03's null 0.06–0.08; pooled bigram 0.09–0.18);
- bigram distance 0.08–0.09 (H-A5-03's null 0.14–0.26; pooled bigram 0.10–0.13);
- overlap with the real lexicon's expanded forms 0.53–0.79 (H-A5-03's null 0.34–0.69);
- `tests/test_benchmark_inputs.py` checks that every pseudo-form uses only first letters and transitions seen in the real lexicon at the same length and position.
The pooled-bigram figures were first given in `docs/benchmark.md` (PR #61) and reproduced by the review of PR #63; the committed script reproduces them.
