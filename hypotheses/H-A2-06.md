## Background
H-A2-07 found that Linear A scribes write distinct fraction signs in one fixed order: C = 1.00 on corpus-v0.1, p < 0.0001. The overview of the Knossos sceptre (Kanta et al. 2025) reports that face δ of the ivory handle (KN Zg 58) has **six different fraction signs in sequence**. This hypothesis predicts their order from the corpus alone, **without using any fraction values**.

## Frozen prediction
Order relations taken from H-A2-07 (`experiments/A2/results/H-A2-07.json`, primary run), majority order of each adjacent pair, then transitively closed. "X > Y" means "X is written before (left of or above) Y".

**One adjustment, made before seeing any sceptre data.** The single E→J pair (ZA 8, already listed as a doubtful reading by Corazza et al. 2021, n. 3) is replaced by J→E. The reason is the 32 JE ligatures in the corpus, in which J is written left of or above E (Corazza et al. 2021, p. 2; GORILA's transcription order). With that adjustment, the graph is acyclic.

**Predicted relations, 42 in total (transitive closure):**
A>B, A>L6, B>L6, E>B, E>F, E>K, E>L2, E>L4, E>L6, E>Y, F>K, F>L2, F>L4, H>K, H>L2, H>L4, J>A, J>B, J>E, J>F, J>H, J>K, J>L2, J>L4, J>L6, J>Y, JE>B, JE>L2, JE>L4, JE>L6, K>L2, K>L4, L>A, L>B, L>E, L>F, L>K, L>L2, L>L4, L>L6, L>Y, L2>L4.

Pairs not listed (e.g. D with anything, B vs K, A vs H) carry **no prediction**.

## Data
- The six fraction signs on KN Zg 58 face δ, **as read and ordered in the first full edition** (*Anetaki II*). If that edition gives the signs as more than one quantity, the primary analysis uses all six in face order, and each quantity on its own is reported as a secondary analysis.
- Sign identifications follow the edition. A sign the edition marks as uncertain is kept, and flagged.
- **Precondition:** this file is merged before anyone on the project reads *Anetaki II*, or the Kanta–Perna fractions paper, for the face δ readings.

## Test
1. **Testable pairs:** every pair of signs (X, Y) on the handle for which the frozen list predicts an order.
2. **k** = how many testable pairs appear in the predicted order. **m** = the number of testable pairs.
3. **Null:** all 6! = 720 orderings of the six signs on the handle are equally likely. p = the share of orderings with as many or more consistent pairs than observed. Computed exactly, by enumeration.

## Decision rule
- If m < 3: **untestable**. Report which signs occurred.
- **Supported:** k/m ≥ 0.9 and p < 0.05.
- **Not supported:** k/m < 0.75 or p ≥ 0.2.
- **Inconclusive:** anything else.

A "not supported" result would mean the handle's scribe ordered fractions differently from the Hagia Triada-dominated corpus. That is itself informative: it could be a different hand, a different register (cult building), or a different system. It would **not** be read as evidence about values.

## What I already know that could bias this
