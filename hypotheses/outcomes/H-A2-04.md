## Step 2 — Comparison
- S = 31 + 65 = **96**
- P = **97**
- No fraction signs are involved.
- **P ≠ S, off by 1.**

## Result: INCONCLUSIVE (by the pre-registered decision rule)
The frozen rule declares the test inconclusive when any quantity involved is damaged or illegible in the GORILA check, and the PO-TO-KU-RO units are only partly intact. If the units are taken as intact, the outcome would be **fail**.

Under neither reading does the tablet add up as written.

## Interpretation (not part of the decision)
- **This does not count against "PO-TO-KU-RO = grand total".** A difference of one unit is the size of a scribal slip or of a misread stroke next to damage.
- **Possible explanations, untested:**
  - (a) a miscount by the scribe;
  - (b) a lost unit stroke in the damaged right edge, either of the 97 itself or of a KU-RO;
  - (c) a reading error in the GORILA drawing or in lineara;
  - (d) a grand total that also covers something other than the two KU-RO subtotals.
- Corazza et al. 2021 (p. 2) cite Montecchi 2009 and 2013 for the general point that "no Linear A inscription containing totals of numeral phrases is without reading or calculation problems". HT 122 appears to be one of them. Montecchi's treatment of HT 122 has not been checked; both papers seem to be paywalled.
- **Lesson for H-A2-01/02/03:** their tolerance rules must say explicitly how off-by-small-integer totals are handled, and must count such cases rather than silently excluding them. This outcome is an example of why.
