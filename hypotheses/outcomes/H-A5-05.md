## What follows (all three pairs of H-A5-03)
- **The revised null works for its purpose.** English's Test 1 false positive (p1 = 0.009) is gone (p1 = 0.288).
- **Test 2 is still needed.** Against length-matched noise, both unrelated lexicons are ahead on Greek words (p ≈ 0.015), and Turkish is ahead on Linear A by about as much (+0.028). Test 1 alone would have come close to "finding" Turkish.
- **M1 is uninformative for all three lexicons.** Hebrew, the language the method was built for, does worse against noise (+0.003) than English (+0.012) or Turkish (+0.028).
- **Not shown:** that Test 2's equal-shift assumption holds in general; that any method would detect a related language at Linear A's size (Tier 2). Nothing here bears on what language Linear A records.
