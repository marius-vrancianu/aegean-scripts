## How strong is this?
- **Most of the information sits in a few repeated pairs.** Of the 25 unordered sign pairs observed, 19 occur only once, and a pair seen once is "consistent" by construction. The permutation null accounts for this, which is why its mean C is already 0.77. The pairs seen two or more times are E→F (5), K→L2 (5), J→B (4), E→L2 (3), JE→L2 (3) and J→L2 (2). All 22 of those occurrences agree.
- **Single source.** The result depends on lineara.xyz's readings and on its ordering of signs within a quantity. It must be re-run on corpus-v1.0.
- **A test of writing practice, not of values.** It says the scribes kept a fixed order. It does not by itself say that the order was by size.

## Secondary test under Corazza et al. 2021 Table 8 values
**This is a consistency check, not independent evidence:** descending order is Corazza's premise 5 (verified from the PDF; see `docs/references/corazza-2021-notes.md`).
- 30 of 33 valued adjacent pairs are in descending order (all quantities). In undamaged quantities it is 23 of 26.
- 8 pairs were skipped (signs without a Table 8 value: L, Y, JE, DD).

The three non-descending pairs are recorded as exploratory observations, n = 1 each:

| Document | Reading in corpus-v0.1 | Status |
|---|---|---|
| ZA 8 | 2 E J | Corazza n. 3 already lists ZA 8.4 as a "doubtful but possible" E J. Not checked by us. |
| KH 86 | A B B | Corazza Table 1 re-reads KH 86.2 as **A A** (GORILA: A B B). The reading is disputed. |
| HT 34 | 2 H K | Not in Corazza Table 1. Claude could not locate it confidently in the GORILA facsimile, because the tablet's layout does not map onto lineara's line order. **Unverified.** |

If HT 34's H K is a correct reading, it would put H above K = 1/10. That conflicts with Corazza's *tentative* H = 1/16, which they flag as uncertain (§4.4). This is an exploratory lead for H-A2-05, not a finding. It rests on one unverified reading.
