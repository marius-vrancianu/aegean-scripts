## Result: NOT SUPPORTED (by the pre-registered decision rule; corrected 2026-11-06)
Five KU-RO totals were testable under the primary scope S1. Two are exactly consistent, against 0.40 expected by chance.

> **Correction (2026-11-06, independent review, `docs/reviews/finding-002-review.md` M1).** This file first reported p = 0.049 and the label **inconclusive**. That p was a Monte Carlo estimate (SE ≈ 0.002). Only two of the five totals can match any sum, so the exact share of reassignments with ≥ 2 consistent rows is 3!/5! = **0.0500**. The frozen rule says "not supported: p ≥ 0.05", so the label is **not supported**. `census.permutation_test` now enumerates all reassignments for n ≤ 8 (`p_exact`), and the results files were regenerated.
>
> **Deviation from the registered procedure** (noted 2026-11-06 by the author session). H-A2-08 registered a Monte Carlo test (10,000 permutations, seed 20261105), and that procedure, run as registered, gives p = 0.0493, i.e. "inconclusive". The label above uses the exact permutation p instead. That is the quantity the Monte Carlo procedure estimates, and the estimate's standard error (≈ 0.002) straddles the 0.05 threshold, so the Monte Carlo label was an artefact of sampling. The change of procedure is recorded here as a deviation. Both labels mean the same thing in substance: the test was uninformative.
>
> **Power.** With these five totals the smallest attainable p was 0.05 (`min_attainable_p`). With any five distinct totals, "supported" (p < 0.01) needs all five exact. The test could not support the hypothesis on this corpus. "Not supported" here means **uninformative**, not evidence that KU-RO is not a total.

## What this does and does not say
- **Most totals cannot be tested at all as transmitted.** Under strict, pre-registered rules, 32 of the 37 KU-RO occurrences are untestable:
  - 23 because the total or its entries touch damage;
  - 7 because fraction signs are involved;
  - 2 because no total survives.

  Since the damage standard was deliberately cautious, some of the 23 may be fine on the clay. This is the main result. It quantifies, reproducibly, the literature claim that hardly any Linear A total is free of problems (Corazza et al. 2021, p. 2, citing Montecchi 2009 and 2013).
- **Where totals can be tested, they mostly behave like totals.** Two match exactly and one is off by a single stroke. With n = 5 the test had no power to reach the registered threshold (see the correction above). Reading "not supported" as "evidence that KU-RO is not a total" would be wrong.
- **The two inconsistent cases look like problems with the scope rule, not with arithmetic.** These are possibilities for discussion, not findings:
  - **ZA 15b:** a ruled line separates a one-entry heading from the KU-RO. That total probably covers entries elsewhere (the other side?), which S1 cannot see. The GORILA check flagged this before the sums were computed.
  - **HT 118:** S1 takes all seven entries above the KU-RO, which sum to 50 against KU-RO 30. A plausible reading is that the KU-RO covers only part of the list (a sub-list, or one commodity). That cannot be tested without a commodity-based scope rule. A rule chosen now, after seeing this, would be post hoc. It can only be registered as a new hypothesis and tested on other data.
- **The alternative scope S2 (all entries on the side) does worse:** 0 consistent, 1 near miss, 3 inconsistent. That supports the primary rule as the better default.
- **PO-TO-KU-RO:** HT 122 is untestable because of damage (consistent with the H-A2-04 outcome). HT 131b involves fractions.

## Caveats
- **Single source.** This is corpus-v0.1, built from lineara.xyz only, and the GORILA checks were made by one LLM reader counting strokes. A second human check of the five testable rows, and of a sample of the 23 "damaged" ones, would be the cheapest way to strengthen this.
- **Departures from the registered protocol** are recorded in `experiments/A2/gorila-checks/README.md`: scope entries were judged as a group rather than one by one, and every number was visible to Claude during the checks.
- **Not yet compared with Montecchi 2009 and 2013.** Those papers have not been obtained. The comparison is planned as an external check.
