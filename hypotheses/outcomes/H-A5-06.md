## Points from the review of PR #60 (`docs/reviews/H-A5-06-review.md`), seen before the run
- **N1, Test 2 and class counts.** Test 2 re-weights by number of signs, but M2's hit rate depends on the number of consonant classes, and Linear A groups have more classes per sign than the Greek words (among three-sign groups, 26% of Linear A's have two classes against 41% of the control's). A **post hoc, unregistered** re-weighting by sign count × class count (`experiments/A5.17/m2_class_reweighted_test2.py`, output `results/M2-class-reweighted-test2.json`; same pseudo-lexicons) gives difference and p: Hebrew −0.030, 0.921; English −0.031, 0.924; Turkish +0.000, 0.513. It decides nothing and changes no reading.
- **N2, groups that cannot match.** At threshold 1/4 a two-class skeleton matches only two-class forms. Hebrew has none, so its 83 two-sign groups (130 two-class groups in all) can never match: only part of the input carries information for Hebrew. With English and Turkish, the few groups of six or more classes can never match.
- **N4, M2's Test 1 is conservative.** On uniform random strings every real lexicon trails its random lexicons by 0.02–0.04 (`experiments/A5.17/m2_threshold_calibration.py`). An outcome A under M2 therefore says less than one under M1.
- **N5.** The threshold calibration is now committed with its seeds (`experiments/A5.17/m2_threshold_calibration.py`) and reproduces the quoted figures exactly.
- **N6.** "The strictest threshold that allows any edit" holds when both strings have at most four classes, as the lexicon forms do.
- **N7.** M2's permissiveness figures count strings longer than any lexicon form, so they are not comparable with M1's.

## What follows (all three M2 pairs)
- **M2 is uninformative for all three lexicons**, as M1 was. With M2 no lexicon is even above its random lexicons on Linear A.
- **The unrelated lexicons' advantage on Greek words is largely a property of M1.** Under M1 both unrelated lexicons beat length-matched noise on Greek words (p ≈ 0.015); under M2 neither does clearly (English p = 0.139, Turkish 0.589). The bigram-matched null of the next protocol revision (H-A5-09) tests the other candidate cause, letter co-occurrence.
- **Limits:** M2's Test 1 is conservative (review N4), and for Hebrew part of the input can never match (N2). An A under M2 is weaker evidence of uninformativeness than an A under M1.
