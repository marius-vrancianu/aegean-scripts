## Method
**M2** (`aegean.benchmark.m2`, default `M2Rules`):
- a sign-group becomes a string of Dolgopolsky consonant classes from its conventional Linear B values (`SIGN_DOLGO`; every Unicode Linear B value has an explicit class; a pure vowel sign gives nothing; a2 is H; signs with two consonants give both; signs without a Linear B value, *301 included, make the group not eligible);
- it matches if a lexicon form lies within **normalised edit distance 1/4** (Levenshtein distance divided by the longer length);
- no affix menus, no unwritten radicals, no vowel read as a laryngeal, no lexicon-side interchanges.

The classes are Dolgopolsky's (1986) as LingPy's DOLGO model gives them (List 2012): P, T, S, K, M, N, R, W, J, H. They differ from M1's in three places: d with t (T), affricates with velars (K), v with w (W).

**The threshold was fixed before any data** (research-notes 2026-11-18, "method M2"). On 1,000 uniform random strings of Linear B values, not Linear A and not the control: 0 allows no edit; 1/3 saturates (0.93–0.99 of two- and three-class skeletons match); 1/4 is the strictest value that allows any edit at the lexicons' form lengths (2–4 classes). Real-lexicon match rates on those random strings at 1/4: Hebrew 0.27, English 0.55, Turkish 0.46; stratified null 0.30, 0.59, 0.49.

**Eligibility** (counted before registration, no hit rate): 378 of the 545 distinct sign-groups of the primary input; skeleton lengths 2: 130, 3: 162, 4: 59, 5: 19, 6: 7, 7: 1. Control: 881 of 918 words.

## Hypothesis
With the English verb lexicon in Dolgopolsky classes, M2 matches Linear A sign-groups more often than it does with length-stratified matched pseudo-lexicons (Test 1), and by a wider margin than on Linear B words of known Greek meaning (Test 2).

**Prediction, stated in advance: outcome A.** English is unrelated (H-A5-02).

**How each outcome will be read:** A — as intended for an unrelated language. B with p1 ≤ α only — English differs from length-matched noise in a way unrelated to Linear A (as M1's English did on Greek words). Any outcome with p2 ≤ α — a false positive of Test 2 for an unrelated language under M2.
