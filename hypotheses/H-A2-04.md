# H-A2-04 — HT 122: PO-TO-KU-RO equals the sum of the KU-RO subtotals

- **Logged:** 2026-11-04 (the merge commit is the timestamp of record)
- **Task:** A2.5 (test run in A2.6)
- **Layer:** L0 (sign IDs and numerals; no fraction values unless the fallback below applies)
- **Status:** pre-registered
- **Batch:** A2 registration batch 1 = {H-A2-04, H-A2-07}. Running count of A2 hypotheses registered: 2.

## Hypothesis
On HT 122 (sides a and b), the quantity written after PO-TO-KU-RO ("grand total") equals the sum of the quantities written after KU-RO on the same tablet, both sides together.

This is a **sanity check of the data and parser**, not evidence about Minoan. The PO-TO-KU-RO = grand total reading is near-consensus (survey §5). The check is worth pre-registering because a failure would point to a reading, damage or parsing problem that has to be understood before H-A2-01/02/03.

## Data
- corpus-v0.1 @ fc87559, documents `HT122a` and `HT122b`.
- Quantities come from `aegean.arithmetic.parse.parse_document`, taking the entries with `marker` = KU-RO and PO-TO-KU-RO.

## Test
1. **GORILA check (precondition).** Before any arithmetic, compare every KU-RO and PO-TO-KU-RO quantity on both sides with the GORILA facsimile in the lineara.xyz images folder at commit 9c81beba. Record, per quantity, whether the reading is legible and intact: yes, no or partly. Where the facsimile and corpus-v0.1 disagree, the facsimile reading is used, and the disagreement is recorded as a corpus erratum.
2. Let S = the sum of the integer parts of all KU-RO quantities on HT 122a and HT 122b. Let P = the integer part of the PO-TO-KU-RO quantity.
3. **Fractions.** If no fraction sign appears in any of these quantities, compare S with P exactly. If fraction signs appear, do the integer comparison as the primary test. Then report the comparison including fractions as a secondary result under the Corazza et al. 2021 Table 8 values. Those values must first be verified from the PDF.
4. **Tolerance:** none. Exact equality only.

## Null model(s)
None is used for inference. For a single equality between numbers in the tens, a chance match is very unlikely, but it is not meaningful to quantify, because S and P are not random draws. The result is reported as pass or fail, with no p-value.

## Known-script validation
Not applicable. This is a data check. The Linear B validation of the checker is A2.4.

## Decision rule
- **Pass:** P = S, with every quantity involved read as intact in the GORILA check.
- **Fail:** P ≠ S, with every quantity read as intact. Then record a corpus or reading problem to investigate before H-A2-01/02/03 run. A fail is not evidence against the "grand total" meaning on its own.
- **Inconclusive:** any KU-RO or PO-TO-KU-RO quantity is damaged or illegible in the GORILA check. Report P and S anyway, with the damage noted.

## Scope alternatives (counted, reported, not used for the decision)
- (i) KU-RO subtotals on side a only;
- (ii) on side b only.

These are reported only to describe the tablet. They are exploratory.
