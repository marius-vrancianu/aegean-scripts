## Limits

- **Circularity for A8.8.** KU-RO, KI-RO and the names were identified by scholars from the same contextual cues a role classifier uses (position, numbers, Linear B parallels). Scoring a classifier on them measures agreement with that scholarship, not independent truth. Only the labelled pictures are free of this, and they are five items on one tablet by one scribe.
- **Names versus places** rest on Linear B, where the two are told apart partly by Greek word forms.
- **Training split only.** Occurrences on the 90 held-out documents are not listed and were not looked at. A published attestation that is missing here (for example HT 97 for PA-I-TO) is on a document outside the training split.
- **Layout evidence comes from SigLA alone**; lineara.xyz contributes only its item boundaries. A label on a document that SigLA does not have can at best reach grade B.
- **Grade C items are not for scoring.** They are listed so that nobody adds them later unnoticed.
