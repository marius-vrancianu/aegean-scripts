# Cross-source disagreements: lineara.xyz, SigLA, Younger (A1.9)

**Type:** data-quality report. It is not a finding, and nothing in it is pre-registered (Marius, 2026-11-10).
**Inputs:** lineara.xyz @ `9c81beba`, SigLA @ `0c4ef69f`, Younger 2024 (non-HT) @ `05305bc5`; layered witnesses from A1.8; documents grouped by the A1.6 concordance.
**Reproduce:** `python -m aegean.corpus.disagreements`. Every figure below comes from `data/processed/disagreements/summary.json` (committed). The itemised list, which quotes source readings, is `items.jsonl` (gitignored).

## Read this first: what agreement does and does not mean

- **All three sources rest on GORILA**, so their agreement is not evidence that a reading is correct.
- **lineara.xyz was built from Younger's earlier web pages**, so lineara.xyz–Younger agreement is the least independent of the three pairs.
- **SigLA is the most independent**: its readings come with its own drawings of the documents.
- **Younger has no Haghia Triada.** On HT documents only lineara.xyz and SigLA can be compared.

Disagreement locates documents where editing or encoding differs. It does not say which source is right; only GORILA, the photographs or the objects can. My notes on *likely* causes below are hypotheses to check, not conclusions (CLAUDE.md rule 5).

## 1. Coverage

| Documents in… | Count |

- lineara.xyz is the most complete. Most of its 786 single-source documents are Haghia Triada nodules with a single sign.
- **SigLA omits all fractions on some documents** (see §5), and it records **no numeral values at all**.

## 2. Sign identities

Signs of each document were aligned pairwise on base sign IDs. "Agreement" below is over the positions where **both** sources identify a sign.

| Pair | Documents | Agree | Substitutions | Agreement | Variant-only | Unidentified in one | Present in one only (a / b) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| lineara.xyz / SigLA | 647 | 4,224 | 108 | **97.5%** | 32 | 39 | 89 / 410 |
| lineara.xyz / Younger | 496 | 2,416 | 32 | **98.7%** | 33 | 106 | 147 / 238 |
| SigLA / Younger | 339 | 1,633 | 30 | **98.2%** | 9 | 87 | 248 / 101 |

**Systematic differences:**
- **OVIS vs CAP in lineara.xyz.** lineara.xyz has **AB022 (CAP, goat) where both SigLA and Younger have AB021 (OVIS, sheep)**: 14 times against SigLA and 13 against Younger. In the other direction, AB021 against AB022, it happens 4 and 2 times. The lineara.xyz codepoint concerned is `AB022F`, used 15 times. Two otherwise separate sources agree against lineara.xyz here, so a lineara.xyz encoding error (OVISf entered as CAPf) is the most likely explanation. **It needs checking on GORILA before any count of animal logograms.**
- **The man sign.** lineara.xyz uses Unicode's single `A100-102` codepoint (*100 VIR / *102 MUL) where the others have AB100. This is now normalised (`UNICODE_NAME_ALIASES`) and no longer counts as a difference.
- **Composite logograms** are encoded differently: one source writes a ligature as one composite sign, another as its parts. Examples are AB028 vs A517, AB080 vs A561, A805 vs A605, AB131 vs A597 and A628 vs A625. These are mostly encoding differences, not different readings.
- **SO vs A363/A364.** Younger reads SO (AB012) where SigLA, and lineara.xyz once, have the Linear A-only signs A363 or A364. This could be a reading difference, or the same sign identified differently.

**Signs present in one source only:** these are mostly on tablets, and **SigLA has more of them** (410 against lineara.xyz, 248 against Younger). Examples: HT 29 (9 SigLA-only), HT 10 (7), HT 49 (9), TY 2 (most in every pair). *Hypothesis:* SigLA draws damaged or partly preserved signs that the other editions leave out. This is to check on GORILA. It matters for sign-frequency statistics (A1.11) and A3/A4.

**Unidentified in one source, identified in the other:** 39–106 per pair. Younger most often has an identified sign where another source has `[?]`, and vice versa; the counts are in the table.

## 3. Word division

Within runs of identical signs, the word boundaries mostly coincide:

| Pair | Boundaries in both | Only in a | Only in b |
|---|---|---|---|
| lineara.xyz / SigLA | 1,506 | 9 | 47 |
| lineara.xyz / Younger | 617 | 0 | 18 |

SigLA divides more often than lineara.xyz (47 against 9). lineara.xyz never divides where Younger does not. That is consistent with lineara.xyz being derived from Younger.

## 4. Numerals (SigLA has none)

lineara.xyz and Younger were compared on the 135 documents where either has numerals:
- **identical: 117;**
- different: 17;
- same total split differently: 1 (KN Zb 35: 100 + 2 against 102).

Examples of differences:
- MA 4: 123 vs **137**;
- MA 6: 35 vs **45**;
- GO Wc 1: 5 vs **10**;
- MA Wc 5: 4 vs 5;
- KH 11: one numeral more in lineara.xyz;
- numerals present in one source only (KH 101, GO Wc 3, KN Zb 27).

**Consequence for A2: the Haghia Triada numerals have a single source.** Younger has no HT and SigLA has no numerals, so every HT numeral in corpus-v0.1 and corpus-v1.0 comes from lineara.xyz alone. This is the most important caveat in this report for the A2 results. It does not change Finding 002, which already carries the single-source caveat, but it means corpus-v1.0 cannot remove that caveat for HT.

## 5. Fractions

Letters were compared after expanding ligatures, so lineara.xyz's single JE codepoint counts as J, E.

| Pair | Documents with fractions | Identical | Different letters | Different order | Fractions in one source only |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| lineara.xyz / SigLA | 153 | 83 | 8 | 0 | **lineara.xyz 57**, SigLA 5 |
| lineara.xyz / Younger | 83 | 77 | 1 | **1** | lineara.xyz 3, Younger 1 |
| SigLA / Younger | 84 | 17 | 4 | 0 | **Younger 57**, SigLA 6 |

- **SigLA omits the fractions entirely on 57 documents** where the other sources have them, e.g. KH 1, KH 10, KH 11, ARKH 1. SigLA's fraction coverage is incomplete, so SigLA cannot replace lineara.xyz for A2 fractions either.
- **Where both sources have fractions, they agree closely.** Differences: HT 123+124 has one J more in lineara.xyz; HT 30 one D more; HT 60 E present only in lineara.xyz; PE 2 B against B B; KH 96 L A A against L B B; KN 49 L against L6; PH 1 L2 H F K against H.
- **Order:** one document, PH 9 (`Y J J` against `J J Y`). This is the linearisation of stacked fraction signs (Finding 002 review, M3). H-A2-07's order regularity rests on sources that agree in all other compared documents, but the comparison covers only the non-HT documents that Younger has.
- **Ligature encoding:** lineara.xyz uses JE/DD ligature codepoints in 24 of the documents compared with SigLA. After expansion they match SigLA's separate signs. Before expansion they would have looked like disagreements: this is the A2 tokenisation pitfall, now handled by the schema.

## 6. Damage marks

Edges were compared only on words with identical signs, and only where both sources record breaks at all. Most edges are not comparable (lineara.xyz marks breaks sparsely).

| Pair | Left: agree / differ | Right: agree / differ | Who marks more |
|---|---|---|---|
| lineara.xyz / SigLA | 195 / 24 | 163 / 33 | lineara.xyz (all 57 differences are breaks lineara.xyz has and SigLA does not) |
| lineara.xyz / Younger | 172 / 0 | 170 / 0 | none: identical where both mark (not independent) |
| SigLA / Younger | 79 / 5 | 74 / 12 | Younger (all 17 differences) |

SigLA marks fewer breaks than either of the others. Its word-edge codes are therefore not a complete damage record, just as lineara.xyz's are not (DATA_SOURCES).

## 7. Documents and metadata

- **Content tier (A1.6) differs between sources for 67 documents.**
  - The most common pattern is T2 in lineara.xyz and SigLA but T1 in Younger (21 documents): Younger counts a logogram cell as a sign.
  - Next, T1 in lineara.xyz and Younger but T2 in SigLA (14): SigLA has no numerals.

  Tiers take the best source, so these differences do not drop documents (A1.6).
- **Site names (19 documents):**
  - **lineara.xyz assigns the Arkhanes tablets ARKH 1–7 to "Arkhalkhori"** (Arkalochori), against SigLA's Arkhanes. This is a lineara.xyz error; the prefix `ARKH` is Arkhanes and `AR` is Arkalochori.
  - ARG Zg 1 is also "Arkhalkhori" in lineara.xyz, against Younger's Argos.
  - The rest are spellings (Malia/Mallia, Haghios Stehanos/Stephanos).
- **Periods (18 documents):**
  - Mostly notation or precision: MMII vs MM IIB, LMIB vs LM I.
  - **Real disagreements:** KH 100 and KH Wc 2117 (LM IIIA in lineara.xyz, LM IB in SigLA); KH 102 (LM IIIA vs LM I).
- **IDs:**
  - 8 documents are joined differently (A1.6).
  - 2 have angle brackets in one source only (HT Wa 1021bis, PH 29).
  - SigLA's internal metadata ID differs from its key in 7 documents (e.g. KH 87 → "KH 97", PH Wa 52 → "PE Wa 52", KE Wc 2a with a stray backtick).
- **Possible renumberings, not merged:** Samothrace `SA We` vs `SAM We`; Thera fragments `THE fr.1-3` / `THE tab.4-6` vs SigLA's `THE 7-12`.

## 8. Values (L1)

Younger's printed values differ from the uniform L1 only where that is by design. He prints conventional values that Unicode leaves unassigned: PA3 (13 cases), ZU (11) and PU3 (5), against L1's *56, *79 and *314. Other differences are logogram labels (VINa, OLE, FIC …) against L1 numbers. No Younger syllabic value contradicts Unicode Linear B.

**SigLA's sign table** gives AB072 the value `ko`. Unicode Linear B gives `pe`, and SigLA also gives `ko` to AB070, so this is probably a SigLA error. L1 is unaffected, because it uses Unicode.

## What this means for the corpus

1. **HT numerals and HT fractions rest on lineara.xyz alone in practice**, since SigLA's fraction coverage is incomplete and it has no numerals. A2's single-source caveat stands for HT. The 17 numeral differences outside HT are a manual-check list.
2. **Check lineara.xyz's OVIS/CAP (AB021/AB022) on GORILA** before counting animal logograms.
3. **SigLA's extra signs on tablets** may be damaged signs that the others omit. Sign-frequency work (A1.11) should report results with and without SigLA-only signs.
4. Agreement rates of 97.5–98.7% on identified signs are high, but **they are agreement between editions, not accuracy**.
