# A2.2 inventory: classification rules (provisional)

Generated by `python -m aegean.arithmetic.inventory` on corpus-v0.1 @ fc87559. Output: `inventory-corpus-v0.1.csv`, one row per marker occurrence.

**Nothing in this inventory adds numbers up, and no fraction values are used.** The classes say whether a total *could* be checked, not whether it checks out.

## Candidate scope
For a marker on line *n*, the candidate scope is every unmarked entry after the previous marker in the same document (or after the start of the document), up to line *n*.

This is a placeholder rule, not the scope rule for testing. The real scope rules (sections, commodity changes, a/b sides, PO-TO-KU-RO over KU-RO subtotals, KI-RO relations) are written into the hypothesis files and frozen before any total is evaluated.

## Classes (first rule that applies)
| Rule | Class | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | indeterminate | The marker has no quantity on its own line |
| R2 | indeterminate | No entries before the marker on this side. Typical of headings, or of totals whose entries are on the other side |
| R3 | damaged | The total quantity itself is damaged |
| R4 | damaged | An entry in scope is damaged, or the document has unparsed tokens |
| R5 | complete-scope-unclear | Intact, but there are several markers in the document or unlabelled quantities in scope |
| R6 | complete | Total and every entry in scope intact |

## Known limits
- **Damage flags are incomplete** (see `DATA_SOURCES.md`, lineara.xyz). A row marked `complete` can still be damaged on the tablet. Example: HT 104 lines 3–4 lie in a stippled band in the GORILA facsimile but carry no flag. Conversely, `damaged` often means edge damage that does not touch the numbers. Every row used in a test must be checked against the GORILA facsimile first, and that check recorded.
- Line breaks follow lineara's entry segmentation, not always the physical lines.
- The source is single (lineara.xyz). Readings are not yet reconciled with SigLA or Younger (A1.9).
